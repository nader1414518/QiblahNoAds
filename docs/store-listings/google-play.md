```
Al-Qiblah & Prayer Companion is a clean, completely free app to help Muslims stay connected to daily worship — with no ads, no subscriptions, and no clutter.

FIND THE QIBLAH INSTANTLY
• Real-time compass pointing toward the Kaaba in Makkah
• Visual guidance when you are facing the Qiblah
• GPS or manual city selection when location is unavailable
• Compass calibration hints for better accuracy

ACCURATE PRAYER TIMES
• Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and Sunrise for your location
• Countdown to the next prayer in your city's timezone
• Multiple calculation methods (Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi)
• Shafi and Hanafi Asr options
• Fine-tune times by minute to match your local mosque
• Prayer reminders you can enable per salah
• Offline support — prayer times cached for the current month

AZKAR & TASBIH
• Morning, evening, and post-prayer supplications from Hisn al-Muslim
• Arabic text with English translation and authentic source references
• Digital Tasbih counter with haptic feedback at 33 and 100

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Simple tab navigation: Qiblah, Prayer Times, Azkar
• Dark mode support
• Available in 24 languages
• Settings accessible from every screen
• Privacy-first: location and preferences stay on your device

PERMISSIONS
• Location — used only to calculate Qiblah direction and prayer times for your area
• Notifications — optional prayer-time reminders

100% FREE. NO ADS. NO PAYWALLS.

May this app be a benefit on your path of worship. If you find it helpful, please leave a review.
```
